Thailand

A country of southeast Asia on the Gulf of Thailand. Once part of the Khmer empire that ruled the entire Mekong River valley, the area was controlled after the 15th century by the neighboring kingdoms of Siam and Annam.A republic in Southeast Asia.

Japan

Japan means a hard smooth and glossy black coating. An example of japan is the enamel like coating used to make metal surfaces black and shiny.A constitutional monarchy on a chain of islands off the E coast of Asia: main islands, Hokkaido.

Malaysia

A country of southeast Asia consisting of the southern Malay Peninsula and the northern part of the island of Borneo.A constitutional monarchy in SE Asia: a federation, comprising the former British territories of Malaya, Sabah, and Sarawak.

'Nation of Brunei, the Abode of Peace'), is a country located on the north coast of the island of Borneo in Southeast Asia.A sultanate under British protection on the NW coast of Borneo: formerly a British protectorate; gained independence 1984.
